# Interviews

Target: 8 to 10 conversations with farmers, cooperative managers and farmer promoters in the seven Eastern districts, by Wednesday 29 October 2026. Each interview fills one block below. `docs/PROBLEM.md` summarises them.

Rules:

- Ask for consent before the first question, and again before using any quote. Write down which it was.
- No names without written or recorded permission. Use a role and district instead ("cooperative manager, Kirehe").
- Write answers in the person's words where you can. Translate quotes into English and keep the Kinyarwanda original beside them.
- Never round or improve a number someone gave you. If they were unsure, write that.

Consent to read out (the team writes the Kinyarwanda version):

> We are students building a free phone tool that helps cooperatives decide whether to sell maize and beans at harvest or store them. We would like to ask you about 15 minutes of questions about your last harvest. You can stop at any time. We will not write your name unless you allow it. May we use short quotes from you in our competition entry for NISR?

## Questions

1. Role, district, crops, and roughly how many members or how much land.
2. Last Season A or Season B harvest: how many kg of maize and beans?
3. When did you or the cooperative sell? How soon after harvest? At what price per kg, and to whom?
4. Why then? (Needed cash, no storage, fear of losses, buyer came, price, debts, school fees, food.)
5. How do you store grain today? (Home bags, hermetic or PICS bags, cooperative store, warehouse, none.) How long?
6. How much did you lose in storage last time, and to what? (Weevils, moisture, rats, theft.)
7. Do you borrow around harvest? From whom (tontine, SACCO, bank, relatives)? At what cost?
8. Where do you get prices today? (eSoko, radio, buyers, neighbours, market visits.)
9. Show Hunika on a phone. Ask them to get a verdict without help. Time it. What was confusing? Would they trust it? Would they share it on WhatsApp?
10. Anything else they want the tool to do.
