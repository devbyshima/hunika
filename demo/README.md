# Demo video

Recorded: [Hunika App Solution For Farming Practitioners](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UFtekibGQqM) on YouTube, 8:40 long. The plan it was recorded from:

1. The problem: the harvest price drop, the end of farm-gate price controls, and a real interview quote only if the person consented. We could not do the interviews, so there is no quote.
2. Decide: Gatsibo, 5 tonnes of maize, hermetic bags: "Store for 2 months". Then Kayonza: "Sell now", because the low case loses more than 5%. Point at the two bars, the risk meter and the price line, then open "More options" to type today's price or your own storage costs.
3. Share from the answer: the Kinyarwanda card and the WhatsApp text.
4. Plan: the district map, a price path, and the SAS production change for one district.
5. Proof: +14,443 RWF per tonne with hermetic bags, the 20-square grid (95% as good as or better than selling), +80,241 when it said store, 46,847 saved with home bags, and every season as a bar, losses in red.
6. The data (NISR SAS 2018-2026, CPI, WFP prices, CHIRPS rainfall), what model v2 tried, and phase 2.

The live verdicts change when the pipeline is rerun with new prices. Check the Nyagatare and Kayonza cards on the day of recording.
