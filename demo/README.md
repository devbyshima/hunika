# Demo video

Not recorded. The team records `demo/hunika-demo.webm` (or a gif) at phone size in Chrome's device toolbar, three minutes at most.

1. 0:00-0:20 The problem: the harvest price drop, the end of farm-gate price controls, and a real interview quote only if the person consented. No quote is in the repo yet.
2. 0:20-1:20 Decide: Gatsibo, 5 tonnes of maize, hermetic bags: "Store for 2 months". Then Kayonza: "Sell now", because the low case loses more than 5%. Point at the stale-price warning and the province-estimate label, type a price in "Today's price", and open "Your costs" to show a manager can use their own bag price or warehouse loss.
3. 1:20-1:40 Share: the Kinyarwanda card and the WhatsApp text.
4. 1:40-2:10 Plan: the district map, a price path, and the SAS production change for one district.
5. 2:10-2:40 Proof: +14,443 RWF per tonne with hermetic bags, 95% of decisions at least as good as selling, +80,241 when Hunika said store, the home-bag losses it avoided (-46,847), 2.8 million RWF a season for 100 t of beans, and the losing seasons in red.
6. 2:40-3:00 The data (NISR SAS 2018-2026, CPI, WFP prices, CHIRPS rainfall), what model v2 tried, and phase 2.

The live verdicts change when the pipeline is rerun with new prices. Check the Nyagatare and Kayonza cards on the day of recording.
